Publications

Journal Papers

  • M. R. Masud, B. Treves, M. Faloutsos. "Disambiguating usernames across platforms: the GeekMAN approach." Social Network Analysis and Mining (SNAM), 2024. [Link]

Conference Papers

  • M. R. Masud, Q. Zhang, M. Faloutsos. "MAGNET: A software-engineering view of the malware ecosystem on the GitHub network." IEEE/ACM International Conference on Advances in Social Networks Analysis and Mining (ASONAM), 2026. New

    A software-engineering perspective on how malicious and dual-use repositories are created, forked, and evolve across the GitHub network.

  • M. R. Masud, M. O. F. Rokon, Q. Zhang, M. Faloutsos. "MetaSim: A search engine for finding similar GitHub repositories." IEEE International Conference on Software Maintenance and Evolution (ICSME), 2024. (Short Paper) [Link] [Demo]

    A systematic study of the origins and motivations behind the creation of malware repositories on GitHub.

  • M. R. Masud, B. Treves, M. Faloutsos. "GeekMAN: Geek-oriented username Matching Across online Networks." IEEE/ACM International Conference on Advances in Social Networks Analysis and Mining (ASONAM), 2023. [Link] [Presentation]

  • B. Treves, M. R. Masud, M. Faloutsos. "RURLMAN: Matching Forum Users Across Platforms Using Their Posted URLs." ASONAM, 2023. [Link]

    An ensemble of traditional and emerging methods to identify off-site accounts belonging to forum users.

    A URL-based user profiling mechanism to extract maximal information about forum users.

  • M. O. F. Rokon, R. Islam, M. R. Masud, M. Faloutsos. "PIMan: A Comprehensive Approach for Establishing Plausible Influence among Software Repositories." ASONAM, 2022. [Link]

    A graph- and embedding-based approach to analyze the influence among software repositories.
