About Me

I recently completed my Ph.D. in the Department of Computer Science at the University of California, Riverside (UCR), where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Michalis Faloutsos. My PhD research sits at the intersection of machine learning, software engineering, and cybersecurity, where I built ML-driven systems that make open-source communities safer and more transparent by analyzing developer behavior, code similarity, and how software evolves.

Across my Ph.D. I designed systems for repository search (MetaSim, RepoScope), cross-platform identity linkage (GeekMAN), and malware ecosystem analysis (MAGNET), with publications at venues including WWW, ICSME, ASONAM, and SNAM. Before my Ph.D., I spent two years as a software engineer building production systems, which shapes how I approach machine learning today.

I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET).

My current research interests span machine learning, large language models, reinforcement learning, alignment, post-training, and model evaluation. I am currently seeking postdoctoral and ML/AI industry positions where rigorous, production-minded work drives real-world impact.
